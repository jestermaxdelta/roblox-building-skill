The 14 patterns
Stat or income per second → multipliers → rebirth is the core of every 2025–26 mega-hit and of the +1, Incremental and cozy-management formats.
One new action on top: steal, grow, kick, throw, climb, bike, draw routes. Remixes with a new action far outperform reskins (inference).
A visible journey beats a number on a menu (inference). Bike For Egg, Climb For Eggs and Depth Incremental lead their formats; the menu versions sit under 2K.
The collection is the multiplier: pets, staff, fruits and cards with rarities, usually 4–5 per egg with the top one around 0.1%.
Merge or fuse tiers (3 into 1, or 4 into 1) reuse the same assets for more rarity levels.
Timers everywhere: spawns every 5/15/30 min, restocks, daily shops, weekly boutiques.
Offline progress gives a reason to return tomorrow.
Weekly updates on a fixed day. Most all-time CCU records landed on update or event days.
Live events are the biggest single lever. Admin abuse, concerts and crossovers pushed the 47.3–47.4M platform record, Steal An Egg's 14.31M, and its 713K → 1.09M concert rebound.
Loss aversion through theft (base locks, alerts) drives session length and defence spending, but brings cheating and bad press.
Mostly dev products, not passes. Venom has 36 dev products, Gun Forge 123, My Small Business 39. LINE UP! and Run A Farm! have no passes at all. Repeat spending feeds the spend-days signal.
Playable within a minute, with no long tutorial.
Free boosts for social actions: like + favourite + group join, a friend boost of +10–20%, private servers.
Small servers with a plot each for management and tycoon games (6–8 players); larger shared lobbies for +1 and steal games.
Retention benchmarks (GameAnalytics 2025, third-party): median session 6.6 min. Games with 19–24 min sessions show median D1 11.5% and D7 1.6%. Observed sessions in comparable games: Venom 14.0 min, My Small Business 15.2 min, LINE UP! 20.7 min, Run A Farm! 21.3 min, Fleet Empire 22.7–24.4 min, Sari-Sari Store 26.7 min, +1 Gun Forge 33.8 min.
## Thumbnail and title playbook v2
Winning thumbnails show one number climbing a ladder, in the game's own unit (Size, °F, OVR, $/s), or the odds of the rarest roll ("1 in 444,444,444"). Roblox only rewards that thumbnail if the first minute of the game delivers it, because it ranks thumbnails by qualified plays, not clicks.
How Roblox scores thumbnails (fact)
Home shows 16:9 tiles and uses only slot 1. Roblox measured +7.2% plays from recommendations after the switch (announcement).
Thumbnail personalization rotates 2–5 images hourly per age group, toward whichever gets more qualified plays. It averages +8.5–12% qualified plays (launch, staff tips).
Roblox reduces exposure for copied art or titles, implied free Robux, and thumbnails that don't match the game (policy).
Thumbnail text should describe gameplay, with nothing important on the bottom edge (Creator Hub).
Informal third-party figures (RoWatcher, no sample sizes): tiles show at about 150 px, text under about 40 px is unreadable, and one high-contrast subject gave a 20–40% click lift.

What the patterns mean (inference)
Feed age splits the strategy. Established games (MM2, DOORS, Phantom Forces) use a logo and moody art because the name is the hook. New format games use saturated colour, a giant number and a visible climb because the mechanic is the hook.
"1 in X" odds are the RNG and egg version of the giant number. The odds are deliberately absurd: 1 in 100Qi, 1/650 quintillion.
Day 1 → Day N is a second way to show progress, as in Depth Incremental ("Day 1 → Day 100, +$23/s → +$23,382,465/s"), Overboard, and Escape Prison's Day 670.
Management games rarely use hooks. Most are photoreal with no text (Fleet Empire, My Cafe). The few that use one use a price flip, like "$0.05 → $35,279" or "$46.2M SOLD!". So a management game with an incremental-style number would stand out.
Ad tiles print their rating, and many are weak. Ad median is 79%, with 8 of 20 under 70% (The Last Christmas 25%, Mutant Lab 33%, Defend An Egg 66%). The median for non-ad tiles is 92%.
The closest match to the snowball design already exists. +1 Burp Power Every Second shows "STAGE 1 / 16 PETS / 12 WORLDS" on a lane, but it's an ad at 66%. The format is open for a polished entry.
Winter tags have started (The Last Christmas, HOOPERS 🎅, [❄️NEW] Egg Farm Simulator) while about 22 Halloween tags linger. Snow alone won't differentiate the snowball game; its hook has to be the Size ladder.

## How to get recommended
Since the 15 Jun 2026 overhaul, Recommended For You ranks games on whether players who found them there don't bounce, come back on several days across 28 days, play long sessions, bring friends, and spend more than once. Roblox says short-term engagement had been overvalued and had favoured attention-grabbing thumbnails, and corrected for it.

How Recommended For You chooses (fact, Discovery docs, FAQ)
Retrieval. From every eligible game, Roblox builds a candidate set per user. A game needs only a "very low minimum" number of plays from any source to enter.
Ranking. It narrows that set to about 100 games on each user's Home. New games are tested daily on small groups. If players stay, Roblox expands to similar users. If not, it tries different ones.
Which players count. Only players who arrived organically through Recommended For You count. Traffic from ads, curation, search and friends is excluded, and signals are averaged per user, so small games aren't disadvantaged.
Timing. Signals update within 24 h, and impressions lag them by a few days. Saturday is the weekly traffic peak.

### +1 Snowball Size For Eggs (in design)
You train Snowball Size on snow piles, roll down a Stage lane smashing ice gates for Wins, and spend Wins on eggs (pets multiply training) and better piles. Meltdown resets Size for a permanent multiplier. The format norms below come from the +1 Evolution games, mainly Dino, Muscle, Speed Evolve and Venom. Treat them as format norms rather than one game's exact values, since fan wikis leave many numbers out.
The loop
Train: stand on a snow pile. Size rises +X per second, multiplied by pets, group and friend boosts.
Run: roll down the lane and smash every gate your Size beats. Each gate pays Wins, and deeper gates pay more.
Spend: Wins buy eggs, better piles and World teleports.
Advance: clear a World's last gate to open the next World.
Meltdown: Size resets. You keep your pets, Robux piles and passes, and gain a permanent multiplier.
