## The shape of the market

- Steal An Egg leads at 1.14M CCU; about 18 games sit at 60–440K (four evergreens from 2014–2020 beside the 2025–26 hits); below 50K every format has one or two leaders and a long tail under 2K.
- Every mega-hit shares one spine: a stat or income per second → multipliers from a collection (pets, staff, fruits, rarities) → rebirth, plus **one new action or a visible journey** (steal, grow, kick, throw, climb, bike, dig, roll, draw routes). Remixes with a new action far outperform reskins; a number ticking up on a menu is not fun to watch, a visible trip is.
- Waves last weeks: a mega-hit peaks 7–8 weeks after launch, plateaus 2–5 months on weekly updates and events, then falls 90–99% to a 50–300K floor. Single-gimmick hits lose 90%+ within 1–4 months. A clone wins only if it ships 2–8 weeks into the parent's rise; format clones get about two weeks of attention.
- Saturated: "Steal a ___" (40 results, 7 above 10K). Crowded: "___ For Eggs" without a journey, "X Incremental" (30 of 40 under 1K), standalone RNG (winner-take-most), "Sell a Million ___" and "Run a ___" names. Rising or forming: cozy management, ragdoll/physics progression hybrids, liminal anomaly horror, fishing (up 55% year on year), physics sports.
- The December holidays are the best window: 1.8–2.5× players and 2.5–3.5× revenue from about 15 Dec to 5 Jan. Halloween lifts horror about 4× on 28–31 Oct. Launch on a Saturday or holiday.

## The 14 shared patterns (use as a design checklist)

1. Stat or income per second → multipliers → rebirth.
2. One new action on top of the idle core.
3. A visible journey beats a number on a menu.
4. The collection is the multiplier: 4–5 items per egg or tier, top item about 0.1%.
5. Merge or fuse tiers (3 into 1, 4 into 1) reuse assets for more rarity levels.
6. Timers everywhere: spawns every 5/15/30 min, restocks, daily shops, weekly boutiques.
7. Offline progress, capped and stated, gives a reason to return tomorrow.
8. Weekly updates on a fixed day; most all-time CCU records land on update or event days.
9. Live events are the biggest single lever (admin events, concerts, crossovers).
10. Loss aversion through theft drives sessions and defence spending but brings cheating and bad press.
11. Mostly dev products, not passes: Venom 36 products, Gun Forge 123, My Small Business 39; LINE UP! and Run A Farm! have no passes at all. Repeat spending feeds the spend-days signal.
12. Playable within a minute, no long tutorial.
13. Free boosts for social actions: friend boost +10–20%, private servers. (House rule: never gate rewards on likes, favourites or group joins; the platform now penalises incentivised engagement.)
14. Small servers with a plot each for management and tycoon games (6–8 players); larger shared lobbies for +1 and steal games.

What separates hits from flops (inference):

- **You do the work yourself in a small 3D space.** Every management game above 5K CCU is hands-on with your own character; menu, map and spreadsheet sims stay under 1K however deep they are (Fleet Empire peaked at 1.8–2K). LINE UP! is the only map-based hit and it plays like a toy (drawing coloured lines), not a spreadsheet.
- You earn within a minute, then hire your way from active play to idle income (Hire 1/5/25 pads; merge 3 of a tier into the next; staff take the place of pets).
- Something to collect: merge tiers, an index, rarities.
- Offline income and a friends bonus; 6–8 player servers with one plot per player.
- Money comes from cheap repeatable dev products more than passes.
- Decorating brings favourites but short sessions; driving controls are hard for young players, keep driving optional.
- Management thumbnails are mostly photoreal with no text; the few with a hook use a price flip ("$0.05 → $35,279", "$46.2M SOLD!"). A management game with an incremental-style number ladder stands out.
- Risk: looking too much like My Small Business triggers the near-duplicate penalty; a distinct theme, art and place file clears it.

## Thumbnail and title playbook v2 (13 rules)

Roblox ranks thumbnails by qualified plays, not clicks, so the first minute must deliver what the tile shows. Home shows 16:9 tiles and uses only slot 1 (+7.2% plays after the switch); personalization rotates 2–5 images hourly per age group (+8.5–12% qualified plays, up to +50–56%). Tiles show at about 150 px; text under about 40 px is unreadable; nothing important on the bottom edge.

| # | Rule | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show the first minute of gameplay | The progression players actually get | A boss they never meet |
| 2 | One focal point, at most 5 elements | Giant character with one item | A busy map overview |
| 3 | Big number in the game's own unit | "1,000,000,000 SIZE" with a heavy outline | Small "earn money" text |
| 4 | Show the climb in one frame | NOOB → GOD, Day 1 → Day 100, $1/s → $9.8M/s | Only the end state |
| 5 | Readable at 150 px | 3–4 words, top or middle | Key text on the bottom edge |
| 6 | Colour to genre, or break it on purpose | Saturated for sims; a number-style tile for a management game | Bright art for horror |
| 7 | A face sells the emotion | Shock, crying, screaming | A blank standing avatar |
| 8 | Fill all 5 personalization slots with different hooks | Ladder, odds, VS, rare item, gag; keep each 7+ days | 5 recolours, or resets every 2 days |
| 9 | Title = [STATUS/emoji] + verb phrase, refreshed every update | `[🎄 UPD] +1 Snowball Size For Eggs` | Halloween tags in late November |
| 10 | Copy the format, not the art | Same formula, your own theme | Cloned art or names |
| 11 | Show your rarest odds only if they are real | "1 in 50,000,000", same odds shown in-game | Odds that don't exist |
| 12 | Put the live boost in the title tag | `[2X SIZE]`, `[⏳6H EVENT]`, rotated with events | A permanent fake "2X" |
| 13 | Don't run ads until the rating is 85% or higher | Wait for the rating to settle | Paying to show "66% Rating" (ad median is 79%; non-ad median 92%) |

Feed stats (coded from 72 + 177 tiles): [TAG] titles 53–64%, emoji 39–52%, giant number 22–37%, before → after 8–16%, "1 in X" 3–10%, no text at all 27–31%. Established games use a logo and moody art because the name is the hook; new format games use saturated colour, a giant number and a visible climb because the mechanic is the hook.

## Discovery facts that change design decisions `[VS]`

- Since 15 Jun 2026 Recommended For You rewards: no bounce in the first 1–3 min, return days across 28 days (D1, D2–7, D8–28), long sessions (capped at 60 min per user per day), intentional co-play (joins, invites, private servers; matchmaking does not count), and repeat spending (spend days beat one big purchase). Only organic RFY arrivals count, averaged per user, so small games are not disadvantaged. Signals update within 24 h; impressions lag by days. Saturday is the weekly peak.
- New since 2026: deep play-through rate (9 Mar), "Not interested" feedback (26 Mar), a 7-day qualified-sessions version (26 Mar), the 12 Oct measurement change (creators report 26–40% PTR drops; do not compare across it).
- Penalties: near-identical visuals, titles or place files (the first to add unique metadata wins); leading with giveaways or Robux; thumbnails that do not match the game; a custom thumbnail is required to appear at all.
- Gameplay video autoplays in RFY since 29 Jul 2026 (up to +39% playtime); real gameplay only. Continue row: the 2 most recent games take the first 2 slots.
- Accounts without an age check get a Kids-equivalent experience (no chat, nothing above Mild). 64% of daily users were age-checked in Q3 2026, 80% in Australia.
- The new-game gate: 250 unique plays from engaged, age-checked 16+ players within 60 days before Kids (5–8) and Select (9–15) can see the game; creator verified with 2FA; all-ages publishing needs Plus/Premium for 2 consecutive months or a refundable fee; expedited review is a refundable 50,000 R$. Plan to bring older players first (Discord, TikTok).
- Standout Games picks 4 per row, mostly small or mid-sized games, on D1/D7, growth, originality, monthly updates and experience events; spending does not count; owners can nominate before launch.
- Benchmarks (GameAnalytics 2025, third-party): median session 6.6 min; sessions of 19–24 min show median D1 11.5% (top 2% 32.3%) and D7 1.6% (top 2% 8.5%). Daily playtime median 16.8 min, top 10% 64.9 min. Use these, not the unsourced 20–35% D1 figures.
