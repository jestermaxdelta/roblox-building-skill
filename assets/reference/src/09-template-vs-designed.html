Why the left one reads as a template

Nothing in it was decided: every value is what Studio inserts. Players have seen these exact greys, this font and these 1 px borders in hundreds of free models, so the game inherits their reputation before it is played.

What "designed" means here

Not more decoration. Fewer, repeated decisions: one radius family, one outline weight, one filled action, states that tell the player where they are. Thin saturated strokes on dark translucent panels and a panel behind every number are what reviewers now call the AI look. Copy this method, not this skin: each game derives its own tokens.
