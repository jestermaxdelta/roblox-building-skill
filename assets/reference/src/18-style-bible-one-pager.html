Tidewater Market: style bible v32026-11-11 · owner: art lead · example filled from the template

Fantasy in one line

Run a stall in a floating harbour market.
Mood words: warm chunky breezy
Three decisions: chunky-rounded shapes, a teal and sand palette with one mustard accent, the rope-knot motif.

Golden sample

Fishmonger stall. Every new asset is held next to it in Studio, under the game's lighting.

Shape language

Chunky-rounded. Primary : secondary : detail = 60 : 30 : 10. One primary mass, 2–4 secondary shapes that break the outline, detail only in the hand band.

Palette (hex)

Base 10

Accents 2: only on things players can use

Value rule

Ground mid-dark (V 35–60), walls one band away, interactables brightest and most saturated. Checked in greyscale.

Proportions against the R15 dummy

Door 5 × 8 studs · counter 3 · step rise 1 · handle thickness ≥ 0.3
Handles and lamps × 1.5–2.5, window frames and trims × 2–4.

Bevel width by viewing band

Seen from 15 studs 0.08 · seen from 40 studs 0.2. Weighted normals always; outer corners only.

Grid and pivots

Architecture 8 studs · props 4 studs. Kit pivots at the front-lower corner; floor props bottom-centre.

Texturing method

Palette atlas (one 256² ColorMap) for props, stalls and NPCs. Flat colour for large architecture. Unique bakes: 2 assets, ≤ 1024².

Material response

Roughness 0.6–0.9 (painted wood, canvas). Metalness 0 or 1. Emissive only on lanterns and pickups.

Wear and story

Light edge wear on wood; no grime. Sails and awnings are never dirty.

Lighting preset

LightingStyle Realistic · ClockTime 17.6 · Brightness 2.2
Atmosphere D/O/H/G 0.35 / 0.2 / 1.5 / 0.4

Smallest readable detail

0.25 studs at 40 studs. Anything smaller is deleted or enlarged.

Banned

Unlisted colours · unbevelled boxes · baked text · one-off textures · neon as decoration.

Sign-off: every asset passes the acceptance checklist under this lighting, on a phone, scored 8 or more of 10 by someone who did not build it.

How to use this sheet

It is a filled example of the template in art-direction-and-quality-bar.md §6, not a house style. Replace every underlined value. Write the bible from a finished golden sample, not from intentions.

Why one page

Constraints applied everywhere beat detail. A page fits beside the viewport, so two artists (or two agents) build one world instead of two.

Where the numbers come from

Bevels and smallest detail from the viewing-distance tables; door, counter and step from the avatar metrics; lighting from the golden-hour preset.
