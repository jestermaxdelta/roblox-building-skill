How to count

Three reads: read 1 holds at most 3 elements, read 2 4–6, the rest is behind one tap. Count groups: one menu block is one element.

Litmus test

Screenshot at phone size over busy gameplay; make a copy with a 4 px blur and one in greyscale. What stands out must be your intended top three. In A and B nothing wins.

Density is allowed

Hit simulators run dense HUDs. Keep the cap by grouping, hide secondary buttons behind More, badge only what is claimable now.
