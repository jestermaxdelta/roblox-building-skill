Test in the darkest and the brightest zone of the map

An additive-only effect tuned in a cave disappears at noon: additive layers can only add light. The dark backing layer is what keeps a shape on a bright floor.

Shape rules

One primary shape a player could draw from memory: crescent, ring, bolt, pillar, dome.
Direction is information: sparks follow the swing; rings expand from the cause.
Repeats differ: later copies of a layer are shorter and changed, never identical.
Grandeur comes from escalating a few layers and cutting others, not from adding everything.
Check the character's silhouette at game distance after each addition.
