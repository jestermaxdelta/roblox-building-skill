Value stack

Thin white-hot core, element colour as the body, one saturated dark layer behind (blue-black or deep red, never grey).

Triangles

20–300 per mesh piece; a full effect under about 1,500. Each layer is its own mesh so it fades alone.

Readability

One ground piece at a time, at most 3 large pieces, at most 35% of the screen (55% for an ultimate).

Low tier

Every effect ships a version with 2–3 layers: keep primary and flash, drop tertiary first. Frame 1 of any flipbook must read as a still.
