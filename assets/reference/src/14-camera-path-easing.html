A spline's parameter is not distance

Dots at equal steps of the spline parameter bunch where waypoints are close and spread where they are far, so the camera lurches. Sample the curve into a length table and move by distance travelled instead.

Rules from the guides

Sine InOut is the default for camera moves and menus; Linear only for things that represent time.
Cutscene paths hold a constant speed between eased ends; prefer a cut to a whip pan. Reduced motion: cuts and fades only.
Drive a path from one loop with TweenService:GetValue(alpha, style, direction); never chain tweens on Completed.
Follow cameras smooth with alpha = 1 − exp(−rate × dt), rate 5–12 / s: the same at 30 and 144 FPS.
FOV moves are envelopes that end at exactly 0: dash +9° in 60 ms, out over 250 ms; summed kicks clamp at +12°. Scope zoom: instant or under 0.15 s.
