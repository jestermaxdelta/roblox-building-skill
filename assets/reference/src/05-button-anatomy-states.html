About ten instances: right for 5 HUD buttons, wrong for 300 list cells (use the flat recipe or a 9-sliced image there).

The full state set: draw and build all eight before shipping the component

Press and release on a millisecond axis (UIScale of the face)

Perception bands

< 0.1 sreads as instant
0.1–0.2 ssnappy: feedback, exits
0.2–0.35 svisible: entrances
0.35–0.6 sdeliberate: big reveals only
> 0.6 sthe player is waiting

Reduced motion

Squash and lift become a colour or brightness change. Feedback stays; movement goes. The response is local and inside 100 ms; the server result reconciles afterwards.
