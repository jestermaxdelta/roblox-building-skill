Frame maths

50 ms is 3 frames at 60 FPS but 1–2 at 30 FPS. A stop that must be felt needs ≥ 66 ms; cap any stop at 200 ms; at most 140 ms of stop per rolling 0.6 s.

Sound against picture

0–30 ms late fuses with the picture. 20 ms early is noticed. 80+ ms late reads as lag. Trim reactive sounds to an onset under about 5 ms.

When it feels long, cut

Each phase is shorter than expected. Cap whole effects near 1.4 s, ground pieces 2.2 s, and keep linger at very low brightness.
