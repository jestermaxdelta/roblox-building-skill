Example timeline: a rare-tier reward with a coin payout (ms from the tap)

Layers overlap: start the next at about 60% of the previous one, never chain on completion. Sound lands 0–30 ms after its picture; 80 ms late reads as lag.

Common0.3–0.5 s

Rare1.0–1.5 s

Each tier adds a layer; it does not only get longer or louder. Keep reward animation under about 10% of play time; repeats and auto modes use a compact reveal.

Reduced motion

Fly-to-counter becomes one colour pulse on the counter, which still counts up. Rays and shake are static; tier colour, banner and sound stay. Panels fade in 0.1–0.15 s.
