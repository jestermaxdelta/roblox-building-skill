Distance = frame height ÷ (2 × tan(FOV ÷ 2)); the guide's table also lists cowboy (5 studs), medium close (3) and extreme wide (50+), and FOV 30 and 50. Lens defaults: establishing 55–70, action 45–60, dialogue and reveals 35–45, close-ups 25–40. A close-up at FOV 70 puts the camera 1.4 studs from the face: distorted, and one step from clipping through it. Between shots of one subject, change size by two steps and angle by 30° or more.

Field of view by screen shape

Vertical FOV is fixed (default 70°, clamped 1–120), so width changes by device. Compose inside the centre 4:3 area and treat the sides as a bonus; check that nothing unfinished shows at the phone's width.

Lead room and headroom

reads Subject on a third, looking and moving into the open two-thirds. Eyes near the upper third line.
cramped Nose against the frame edge, empty space behind, head centred with dead space above.
The arrow is the look or travel direction: leave more space in front of it than behind, keep eyes near the upper third, and do not centre everything. Letterbox bars only while controls are off, capped at 10% of height per bar.

The 180-degree line

Cameras 1, 2, 3: A stays screen-left.
Camera X: A and B swap sides. The cut reads as a teleport.
Draw a line through the two subjects (or along the direction of travel) and keep every camera of the scene on one side of it. Cross it only with a visible move or a neutral shot on the line.

Roblox cutscene rules

Client-only; actors are local clones; game state changes on the server first.
Skip on every scene over 2 s; the control appears after 1–2 s, bottom-right, thumb size.
One restore function for every exit (finished, skipped, died, error, watchdog): camera, FOV, controls, HUD.
Comfort: pans under 30°/s (cut instead of a whip pan), no roll while moving, FOV change ≤ 15° over 0.5 s or more.
Reduced motion: hold each shot at its final framing and cut; no shake, drift or FOV animation.
Near plane: keep the camera 1.5 studs or more from any surface it passes.
