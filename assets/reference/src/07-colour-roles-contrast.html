Colour roles: colour means function, not decoration (example values: every game derives its own)

About 60% of a screen is neutral surface, 30% structure, at most 10% accent: one saturated filled action per screen. Premium is used for Robux actions only. Each destination keeps one colour everywhere. Two more roles are reserved: OnAccent (label ink that passes 4.5:1) and Focus (the gamepad ring, used nowhere else).

Rarity ladder under colour-vision simulation

Protan and deutan views fold green, gold and pink toward the same yellows and blue and purple toward the same blues. The ladder survives because lightness rises every step and every card also carries a name and a pip count.

Risky pairs

red / green · red / brown · green / brown · green / orange · blue / purple · blue / green · yellow / light pink · any two pastels.

Signals fail when they are small (a 6 px pip), far apart, pastel or brief (a 0.2 s flash). Rescue a signal you cannot recolour by making it bigger or longer-lived.

Contrast: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and icons (ratios computed from the swatches shown)

Test over a gameplay screenshot, not a grey canvas. Text that sits on the 3D world gets an outline or a plate, because the world behind it changes every frame.

Two teams: blue versus orange needs a lightness gap and an emblem

Minimum Oklab distance under every simulation: 0.08 big patches side by side · 0.15 small or separated signals (outlines, markers, pips) · 0.20 plus a second channel for critical reads (ally or enemy, safe or harmful floor) · lightness-only check ΔL ≥ 0.15.

Second channels

Every information-carrying colour also uses one of: lightness, shape, icon, text, pattern, position, size, motion. Heal and damage differ by sign and direction; crits are bigger, not just yellower.

Cheapest tests first

1. Greyscale pass: ColorCorrectionEffect with Saturation = −1 (3D only; desaturate UI screenshots in an editor). 2. Simulator pass per graded zone. 3. Numeric audit of the tokens. 4. Smallest size, real phone, outdoors.
