What the picture says

A button 40% from the top is within easy reach on a phone and far outside any reach on a tablet. Place frequent actions relative to the jump button (for example 20 px to its left, 0.8 × its size), never by screen percentage.

Where things go

Easy: the primary action and one or two frequent buttons, outside the reserved zones. Stretch: the menu column, tracker toggle. Out of reach: status only: objective, timer, currencies, toasts.

Limits of this sheet

Reach radii are illustrative (about 45 and 70 mm from the grip), not measured Roblox data. The emulator does not show real thumbs: check on the real 3 GB Android phone and one tablet, with chat open.

Detecting the layout

Phones report short sides of about 360–430 px in landscape, the smallest tablets about 600. A workable phone test: touch input with a short side under about 520 px. Use PreferredInput, not TouchEnabled, to decide whether touch controls show.

Reserved zones

The dynamic thumbstick covers about 20–25% of the left side; a button beside it gets opened by accident. The default jump button is about 70 px on small screens and about 120 px on large ones: read it at runtime. The right half is also the camera-drag area.
