What the picture says

A button 40% from the top is inside the right thumb's arc on a phone and far outside it on a tablet: the arc is the same hand, so it covers under half the fraction of a tablet. Place frequent actions relative to the jump button (centre at jump.X − 0.6 × jump size − 8, size 0.8–1.0 × jump), never by screen percentage.

Where things go

Inside the arc: the primary action left of jump; up to 3 secondary actions on an arc about 1.3 × the jump size from its centre. Right edge above jump: the menu block. Top centre (0.3–0.7 W): status only.

Limits of this sheet

Device sizes and the inner arc are illustrative; the outer arc uses the guide's 0.9 H of a phone, a rough figure. The emulator does not show real thumbs: check on the real 3 GB Android phone and one tablet, with chat open.

Which layout am I on

Roblox itself switches the jump button at a short side of 500 px. Decide whether touch controls show with PreferredInput, not TouchEnabled (touch laptops). Design at Phone-S 800×360 first, then check 667×375, 844×390 and the 1024×768 tablet frame.

Reserved zones

A touch that starts in the left 40% × lower two thirds starts the stick, so buttons there get pressed by accident. Jump is 70 px when the short side is ≤ 500 px (95 and 90 px from the corner), else 120 px (170 and 210 px). Keep an 8 px moat; read live sizes at runtime.
