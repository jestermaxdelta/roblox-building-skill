Proportions of a light attack

Roughly 20–30% wind-up, 10–20% active, 50–70% recovery.

Who gets the anticipation

Player: motion on the input frame; wind-up is input delay, so keep it short and let a glint or sound anticipate. Enemies: telegraph ≥ 14 f (0.45 s), bosses ≥ 15 f, so players can react. PvP moves meant to be reacted to need ≥ 8 f startup (reaction time is about 0.25 s).

Markers drive everything

TrailOn, Hit, TrailOff and CancelOpen are animation markers. Damage, VFX and SFX fire from the same marker, so when the clip is retimed they move together.

Buffers and cancels

Buffer a combo press for 0.15–0.30 s; record on press, never on release. Allow the next hit at the link point and a dodge shortly after the active frames, with a 0.05–0.1 s fade.

Never baked

Hit-stop (none on light hits, 50–89 ms medium to ultimate) is runtime, on confirmed hits, on the attacker's client only. Heavy means more frames, never slower playback.
