Rules

Game icon: 512×512 or larger, shown down to 150 px and smaller. One focal subject filling 60–80%; three or fewer value masses; a flat or two-tone ground.
Pass marks: at 32 px a stranger names the subject; at 64 px they name the genre; in greyscale the subject separates. At 32 px no text survives.
Inventory cell: 64–72 px. HUD currency icon: 28–32 px. An icon must read at the size it ships.
One family: glyph icons drawn white with the outline baked in and tinted; item icons rendered from the real Blender asset with one camera and light rig.
