Open the chest
Shown together for reference only. In a game, one pointer and one pulse at a time: with two, players follow neither. Pick the device from the table, show it for the current step, remove it when the step completes.

When help appears

Timed hint: delay = the time most players need + 1 s (docs example: most act by 10 s, hint at 11 s). It appears once, after a stall, and goes on progress. Guidance stops once the player has done it unaided.

The objective line

Verb + object in 2–5 words; 8 or fewer words on screen at any moment; one- and two-syllable words a 7-year-old reads. The control token is resolved for the device in use.

Fix the space first

Target: about 80% of first-time players find the critical path without UI. If fewer do, change light, openings and path value before adding arrows.
