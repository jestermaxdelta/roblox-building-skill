When help appears

Try first, help later: hintAfter 6–10 s without progress (or "when most players have acted, + 1 s"). Progress resets the timer. Guidance stops once the player has done it unaided.

The objective line

Verb + object, 8 words or fewer, the control named for the device in use (tap icon, E, gamepad glyph). Never over the play area. One step, one line.

Fix the space first
