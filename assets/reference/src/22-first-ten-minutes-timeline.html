Not before minute 10 and a first win

No purchase prompt, no offer popup, no notification opt-in. At arrival: no menu, no play button, no cutscene. Offers begin only through the offer gate, after positive moments.

How to use it

Write your game's beats into this table, build exactly that, then time it with a stopwatch on a 3 GB Android phone. Log each beat as one custom event with seconds since Spawned, which gives time-to-beat as well as the funnel.

Tutorial style

No forced tutorial; at most a soft-gated, skippable guided first loop of 3–7 steps, timed hints for the minority who stall. Teach each system when the player meets it. Hints go only to players who have not acted after most players have.
